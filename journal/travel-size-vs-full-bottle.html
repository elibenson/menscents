Travel spray or full bottle?

A smarter way to choose fragrance size before committing to a large purchase.

Cost per milliliter is not everything

Larger bottles often offer a lower price per milliliter, but that is only a saving if you actually use the product. An unused large bottle is more expensive than a finished travel spray.

Travel sizes make experiments easier

Smaller formats let you wear a fragrance across several days, seasons, and situations without committing to a full bottle. They are particularly useful for expensive or unfamiliar scents.

Check the actual quantity

Travel sprays, discovery sets, miniatures, and samples are not interchangeable. Read the listed volume and whether the container has an atomizer.

When a full bottle makes sense

If you have finished a sample, enjoy the drydown, and reach for the scent regularly, a full bottle may be reasonable. Compare current retailer pricing and verify authenticity first.
