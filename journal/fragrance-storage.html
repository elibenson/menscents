How to store cologne properly

A simple storage routine that protects your fragrance from unnecessary heat, light, and handling.

Choose a stable environment

Keep bottles in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and large temperature swings. A bedroom drawer or closed cabinet is often more suitable than a sunny windowsill.

Think twice about the bathroom

Bathrooms can be convenient, but frequent heat and humidity changes make them a less predictable storage location. A consistently temperate shelf elsewhere is generally preferable.

Keep bottles closed

Replace the cap where supplied, avoid leaving atomizers exposed unnecessarily, and store bottles upright to reduce leaks. Do not transfer fragrance repeatedly between containers without a reason.

Watch for change

Color shifts alone do not prove a fragrance has spoiled. A major change in smell, unexpected cloudiness, leakage, or irritation is a reason to stop using it and investigate.

Buy what you can use

A large collection can be enjoyable, but accumulating bottles faster than you wear them creates storage and aging challenges. A smaller rotation is often simpler.
