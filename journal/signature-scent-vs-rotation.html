Signature scent or fragrance rotation?

How to decide whether one bottle or a small wardrobe fits your lifestyle.

The case for one bottle

A signature scent simplifies decisions, reduces spending, and creates consistency. Choose something you genuinely enjoy across your most common settings, rather than something you hope other people will recognize.

The case for a rotation

A small rotation can help if your daily life spans very different environments: office, outdoor activity, formal events, or evenings out. Each bottle should have a clear role.

Avoid collecting by category alone

Owning five fragrances with similar character is not inherently wrong, but it may offer less practical variety than you expect. Compare what each adds to your existing collection.

A simple decision test

For the next month, record which fragrance you reach for and why. If one bottle handles nearly everything, stay with one. If you repeatedly wish for a different direction, sample a second.
