How to avoid counterfeit fragrance

Practical buying safeguards without pretending a single batch code can prove authenticity.

Buy through reliable channels

An authorized retailer or a brand’s own store is a straightforward way to reduce uncertainty. Other sellers may be legitimate, but the burden of checking provenance and returns is higher.

Be skeptical of extraordinary discounts

Price alone cannot establish authenticity. However, an implausibly low price, vague product photos, and unclear seller identity are reasons to pause.

Batch codes are not a magic test

Packaging details and batch codes can be copied. No single online checker can authenticate every bottle. Look at the seller, invoice, packaging consistency, and the brand’s own guidance together.

Inspect the transaction

Keep order records and photographs if something arrives damaged or suspicious. Use the retailer’s dispute process and contact the brand when appropriate.

Avoid risky marketplaces

Be cautious with listings that use only stock images, have no meaningful return policy, or refuse to identify the seller. The cheapest listing is not always the best value.

Make a considered choice

Explore the fragrance index, try the Scent Finder, or compare two fragrances. These guides are research-led and do not represent hands-on testing.
