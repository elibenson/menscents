How to compare two colognes fairly

A repeatable side-by-side method that keeps hype and brand reputation out of the decision.

Define the job first

Are you comparing daily work scents, evening fragrances, or a versatile all-rounder? A fragrance can win one use case and lose another.

Wear them on separate days

For the most useful test, wear each fragrance on a comparable day with similar weather and activities. Side-by-side wrist tests are helpful for first impressions, but can become confusing over hours.

Score what matters to you

Consider opening, drydown, personal enjoyment, suitability, comfort, price, and how different it is from what you own. Avoid assigning exact longevity scores based on a single wearing.

Ignore the winner narrative

The more expensive fragrance is not automatically better. The less expensive alternative is not automatically identical. Choose the one you actually prefer at the price you are willing to pay.

Keep a short note

A simple note in your phone after each wearing is more useful than relying on memory from a store visit.

Make a considered choice
