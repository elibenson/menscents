How to choose a signature scent.

The best signature scent is not the loudest, most expensive or most complimented. It is the one you genuinely want to wear.

Begin with your actual routine

Think about where you spend most of your time: a shared office, outdoors, in warm weather, or at evening events. Your signature fragrance should suit your real life, not an imaginary one.

Choose a direction, not a brand

Try a few distinct styles—perhaps one fresh aromatic, one dry woody, and one warmer scent. This teaches you more about your taste than sampling five similar bestsellers.

Wear candidates for full days

Do not decide from the opening alone. Try each candidate in a normal day, then revisit how you feel about its drydown, comfort, and suitability.

Keep a simple shortlist

Write down what you liked, what bothered you, and whether you would wear it again tomorrow. If two options are close, buy the smaller size or sample again.

A signature is optional

You do not need a lifelong signature or a recognizable scent trail. One fragrance you enjoy consistently is enough, and changing your mind later is fine.
