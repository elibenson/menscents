How to choose fragrance as a gift

A practical decision framework for gifting scent without guessing someone’s taste.

Start with what they already wear

If you can learn the names of fragrances the recipient enjoys, you have a more reliable starting point than their age or personal style alone.

Avoid overly confident assumptions

Someone who dresses formally may love playful fragrances; someone who wears casual clothes may prefer classic woods. Taste in scent is personal.

Discovery sets reduce the gamble

A set of samples or a retailer gift card can be more thoughtful than an expensive full bottle the recipient feels obligated to wear.

Check the return and exchange rules

Gift receipts, unopened-box policies, and retailer exchange windows vary. Verify the current terms before purchasing.

Make it easy to choose

If buying a bottle, explain why you selected it and make clear that exchanging it is welcome. A good gift should not create pressure.

Make a considered choice

Explore the fragrance index, try the Scent Finder, or compare two fragrances. These guides are research-led and do not represent hands-on testing.
