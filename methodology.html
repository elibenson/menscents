How we make recommendations.

We value transparency, useful context and clearly stated limitations.

Research-led, clearly labeled

Current profiles draw on published fragrance descriptions and editorial synthesis. We do not imply personal wear tests or independently verified longevity.

No fabricated scores

We avoid invented performance numbers, unverified compliments, imaginary test panels, and claims that a fragrance is universally best.

Sample before you buy

Fragrance is subjective and performance varies. We encourage testing the exact concentration and product before buying.

Commerce and independence

Any future affiliate relationships will be disclosed. Commercial relationships should not determine our editorial conclusions.

Corrections

Product formulas, prices and availability can change. We welcome corrections through the site owner’s published contact channel when available.
