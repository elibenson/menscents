He already owns Sauvage.

December’s actual search. Do not send another blue flanker and call it a gift.

If you know he likes fresh and safe, Bleu de Chanel is the lateral move. If you know nothing, buy a sample set, not a 100ml. A wrong full bottle is a polite candle. A three-vial set gets used.

Desk gift Under $50: a barbershop classic or a woody clone, plus a note that says smell it before you commit. Over $100: Bleu, or a niche decant of something woody if he already talks about cologne. Never Sauvage Elixir unless he asked for the stronger one.

Gift sets with lotion and a shower gel are filler. Spend the same money on one bottle he will actually wear, or on samples so he can choose.
