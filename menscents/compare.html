How to choose

Buy Bleu if you need one bottle for work and everything else. Buy Y if compliments matter more than blend-in. Buy Sauvage EDP only if you like the smell and do not mind that the room has met it before. The Elixir is a different, heavier thing. Do not start there.

Smell them first. A 2ml decant costs less than a bad bottle, and these three are the whole argument on most men’s first search.

The set One vial of each, worn on different days. Two sprays on skin, not six on a shirt. If you already know you will not spend designer money, go to the clone desk instead of pretending the sample changed your budget.
