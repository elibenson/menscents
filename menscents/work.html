Nothing that enters first.

The office test is simple. If a coworker can name the bottle from the hallway, it failed.

Wear Bleu de Chanel Eau de Parfum. Two sprays, chest and neck, not the shirt. It reads as clean and slightly woody, and it does not pick a fight with a conference room. Sauvage fails this test. YSL Y is too sweet for a quiet office and fine for a client dinner.

Desk pick Bleu de Chanel EDP if you will own one work bottle. A woody dupe around $30 if you will not. Sample both before you decide the gap is worth it.

What to skip

Elixir concentrations, oud, and anything sold as a compliment monster. Those are night bottles. Also skip body spray layered under cologne. That is how a normal scent becomes a complaint.

Other jobs: under $40, hot weather, a gift for someone who already owns Sauvage.
