About

A desk, not a store.

Menscents tells you what to smell first, then links to the sample, the bottle, or the clone. We do not sell fragrance and we do not ship orders.

The site is published by Menscents. The limited liability company is planned and not yet filed. Until it is, the publisher is the person operating this site.

Recommendations are opinions. Closeness scores are estimates, not lab tests. Prices on the homepage are examples until affiliate accounts are connected.
