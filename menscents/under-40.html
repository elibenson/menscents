Under $40, still there after lunch.

At this price you are buying a clone or a drugstore classic. Both are fine. A watered body spray is not.

Start with Lattafa Asad if you wanted Sauvage Elixir and will not pay for it. Start with Armaf Club de Nuit Intense if the brief was Aventus and you can live with a rough opening. Start with a Dossier-style woody if the job is the office and Bleu is the reference.

Buy rule One bottle, not a five-pack of clones. Wear it a week. The second cheap bottle is how a desk becomes a pile. Full notes live on the clone desk.

Performance at this price is uneven by batch. If it is gone in two hours, it failed, even if the opening was right. That is the only standard that matters under $40.
