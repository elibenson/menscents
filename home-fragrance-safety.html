Home fragrance safety: candles, diffusers and pets

A beautiful room should also be a safe room.

Candle basics

Never leave a candle burning unattended. Use a stable heat-resistant surface, keep flames at least 12 inches from combustible materials, and keep candles away from children and pets. Follow product instructions and extinguish before sleep.

Essential oils are not automatically pet-safe

Concentrated oils may be harmful if swallowed or deposited on skin or fur. Diffusers can also create inhalation concerns. Birds are especially vulnerable to airborne exposures; avoid essential-oil diffusers in homes with birds.

Ventilation and sensitivity

Fragrance can be unpleasant or irritating for some people. Start with less, allow fresh air into the space and stop using a product if it causes symptoms. Do not claim a scented product purifies indoor air.

Keep products secure

Store fragrance oils, refills, sprays and candles away from children and animals. Prevent spills and avoid positioning a diffuser where it can be knocked over.

When in doubt

Consult a veterinarian about exposure risks for your specific pets, and follow the manufacturer’s safety instructions for every product.
