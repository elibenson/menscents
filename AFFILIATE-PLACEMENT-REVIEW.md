# Affiliate placement review — Menscents

## What changed
- Every page has a clearly labeled **Shop on Amazon** navigation link.
- Homepage features four direct, product-specific Amazon search links beside the profile links, with a nearby disclosure.
- Each editorial article has two relevant product search links near the end of the article, based on its topic.
- Directories and interactive tools have a compact shopping option.
- Existing product page Amazon links are preserved and normalized.
- Every Amazon link has the `bnsn-20` tag and `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"`.

## Important limitations
- Amazon links are **search results**, not verified listings or ASIN links. Buyers should confirm product authenticity, size, seller, and price.
- We have not verified the Amazon Associates account, attribution, Program policies, or live purchase tracking.
- The global navigation link is intentionally generic; the contextual links are the primary buying experience.
- Policy and disclosure pages have only the navigation shopping link, to avoid commercial clutter.
- Review the layout on a Vercel preview before publishing, particularly on mobile.
