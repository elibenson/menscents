How many sprays

Two. Maybe three if your skin eats fragrance. In an open office or small meeting room, less is better. Apply to skin, not clothes, and give it 10 minutes before you walk in.

Should you buy the full bottle first?

No. A sample costs a few dollars and tells you whether you actually like it on your skin for a full day. Then decide between the real bottle or a cheaper version in the same direction.
