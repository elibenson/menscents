## Suggested 60-minute review meeting
1. **10 min — voice:** Read the signature-scent and blind-buying guides together. Are they direct, useful, and masculine without sounding performative?
2. **15 min — accuracy:** Spot-check concentration, sampling, authenticity, and storage advice. Flag anything requiring specialist review or citations.
3. **15 min — commercial relevance:** Decide which guides should link to specific profiles and where affiliate links add real utility. Do not add unverified pricing.
4. **10 min — product profiles:** Open the eight existing `bottle/` pages. Mark manufacturer descriptions, performance claims, prices, and comparison language that require verification.
5. **10 min — go/no-go:** Approve individual pages in `editorial/PARTNER-REVIEW.csv`. Keep unapproved pages off the public site until you are comfortable with them.

## Editorial rules
- These are **research-led drafts**, not hands-on wear tests. Do not add first-person testing claims unless an editor has genuinely tested the product.
- Do not claim exact longevity, projection, batch authenticity, fixed prices, or universal concentration percentages.
- Manufacturer notes are marketing descriptions, not proof of chemical ingredients.
- Avoid medical claims and respect fragrance-free policies.
- Verify factual product claims with primary brand sources and dated retailer listings before publication.
- Check rights and accuracy of images, affiliate disclosures, and all external links.
- The 45 product names in `editorial/research-queue.json` remain **research candidates**, not completed product reviews.

## Practical publishing decision
This ZIP contains new guides linked from the Journal for **review on a private Vercel preview**. If you want a public launch before approving all guides, remove the unapproved HTML pages and their index/sitemap links, or publish only approved pages.
