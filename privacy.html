Privacy

What this site keeps.

This is a draft privacy page for v1, dated October 8, 2026. Replace the contact line when the inbox exists.

Affiliate links

Some links will go to retailers such as Amazon and fragrance shops. If you buy, Menscents may earn a commission. Those retailers set their own cookies and privacy terms. We do not see your payment card.

Email

The sample-set form currently stores the address in your browser only, so a refresh on another device loses it. When the list is connected, we will use the address to send the set you asked for and occasional desk notes. You can unsubscribe from those notes.

Analytics

No analytics tag is installed in this version. If one is added, this page will name it.
