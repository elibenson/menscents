# Deploy Menscents

1. Extract the ZIP and upload the contents (not the containing folder) to your GitHub repository.
2. Let Vercel build a preview deployment.
3. Check the homepage at desktop and mobile widths, the Scent Finder, navigation, article links, and outbound Amazon links.
4. Confirm that your Amazon Associates tag `bnsn-20` is active and disclosures are appropriate.
5. Promote the verified preview to production.

No original product photography or first-hand wear testing is claimed. Illustrative editorial images are not actual product photographs.
