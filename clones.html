Lattafa Asad vs Sauvage Elixir

Asad is the one to test if Elixir was the brief. It is sweeter and rougher in the opening, and it does not have the same polish an hour in. For the price, it is the correct first clone. It is not a reason to skip smelling Elixir if you were actually going to buy it.

Armaf Club de Nuit Intense vs Aventus

The famous one, and the roughest. Smoky pineapple, a harsh first ten minutes, then a wearable woody. Batch variation is real. If Aventus is a $400 curiosity, this is the bottle. If you want the smooth niche thing, this will annoy you.

A clean woody dupe vs Bleu

Dossier-style woody dupes get the shape of Bleu and miss the incense. Shorter life, cleaner, fine for an office that does not need the real bottle. This is the under-$40 answer on the work page.
