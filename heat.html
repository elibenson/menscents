Two sprays, then stop.

Humidity amplifies everything sweet, woody, and resinous. Summer is a citrus or an aquatic problem.

Acqua di Giò, or anything in that clean aquatic lane, is the safe hot-weather bottle. A straight citrus cologne is the other. YSL Y can work outside and fails in a closed car. Sauvage Elixir is a cold-night scent. Do not bring it to a July afternoon.

Desk pick One aquatic, one citrus, sampled on skin in the actual heat. If you want cheap, a light blue clone in the Acqua lane beats a sweet Lattafa in August. The office page still applies if the heat is an air-conditioned building.

Application matters more in heat than the bottle does. Two sprays. Skin, not fabric. Reapply at night if you need to, instead of loading up at noon.
